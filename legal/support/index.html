Last updated 2026-11-26. Publisher: Sunny Vashisht.

We reply to every message. Email samayhuf@gmail.com and tell us the app name, your device and what happened.

For billing, subscriptions and refunds on iPhone, Apple handles the payment: use Settings › your name › Subscriptions on your device, or Apple’s “Report a Problem” page. Cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends to avoid renewal. To restore a purchase, use Restore Purchases in the app with the same Apple ID. On Android, use Google Play’s subscription settings.
