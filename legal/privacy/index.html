Privacy Policy

This policy covers apps published by Krosz Inc. that link here. Apps with their own policy page use that page instead.

What we collect

Each app collects only what it needs to work. Depending on the app this can include an email address or name you choose to give us, purchase status, and basic diagnostics. Features that read your photos, camera or location do so on your device and ask for permission first.

Purchases

Payments are processed by Apple or Google. We use RevenueCat to manage subscriptions and see whether a purchase is active. We never see your card number.

Sharing

We do not sell personal data. We share data only with service providers that run the app for us, or when the law requires it.

Your choices

You can revoke permissions in your device settings, and ask us to delete data we hold about you by emailing samayhuf@gmail.com.
