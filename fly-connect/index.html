About Fly Connect

Fly Connect is a network for cabin crew and ground staff from every airline. Follow and unfollow like on Instagram, and chat with audio and video calls.

The theme is bright and light.

Built for aviation crews

Crew work odd hours in different cities. A network of people in the same job makes it easier to stay in touch.

How Fly Connect works

  1. 1

    Create a profile

    Join as crew or ground staff.

  2. 2

    Chat and call

    Message, or use audio and video calls.
