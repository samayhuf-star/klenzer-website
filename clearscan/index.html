About ClearScan

Keptly (ClearScan) is a document scanner with no watermark and no account. Unlimited scans, automatic edge detection and straightening, and many pages in one PDF are free.

Text is read on your iPhone, so you can search inside every scan, pull out facts such as dates and ID numbers, and get reminders before a bill is due or a document expires.

Private by design

Many scanner apps upload your pages to read them. Keptly reads the text on your phone, which matters for IDs, bills and contracts.

Share-safe mode

Hide ID and card numbers before you share a document, so you send what is needed and nothing more.

Deadlines and reminders

Keptly finds due dates, expiries and renewals and lists them with days remaining. Add repeating reminders, export to Calendar, or use the widget.

Receipts and totals

Receipt totals by month show what you spent at a glance.

Keptly Plus (optional)

Optional extras such as auto-sorting scans into Receipt, Invoice, ID and Letter. Everything you already use stays free.

How ClearScan works

  1. 1

    Scan

    Choose Document or Book mode and point the camera. Edges are detected and straightened automatically.

  2. 2

    Clean up

    Pick Auto, Colour, Grey or B&W, rotate, and add more pages to the same PDF.

  3. 3

    Search and review facts

    Find any word on any page, and check dates and numbers found on your phone.

  4. 4

    Share

    Share a PDF, using Share-safe mode when it contains IDs or card numbers.
