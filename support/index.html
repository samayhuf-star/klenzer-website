Support

Last updated 2026-11-26. For the Klenzer app. Seller: Sunny Vashisht.

For help with Klenzer, email samayhuf@gmail.com. Include your device, OS version, Klenzer version and what happened. Please do not send private photos or payment details.

What does it cost?

Klenzer is free to scan and review, with 10 free action items a day. Klenzer Pro removes the daily limit: $5.99/month or $29.99/year with a 7-day free trial, or $29.99 lifetime. Prices can differ by country.

How do I cancel, or restore a purchase?

On iPhone, go to Settings › your name › Subscriptions, or use Apple’s Report a Problem page. Cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends. To restore, open Klenzer and use Restore Purchases with the same Apple ID. On Android, use Google Play subscription settings.

I deleted items on iPhone but the space did not come back

Deleted items go to Recently Deleted in the Photos app. Klenzer cannot empty that album. Open Photos and empty Recently Deleted to free the space.

Do I need an account?

No.
