Quick answers

What is Apps?

Apps is a collection of 19 iPhone and Android apps published by Krosz Inc., 444 Alaska Avenue, Suite #CRY683, Torrance, CA 90503, USA. Each app does one job well, such as freeing up phone storage, helping you sleep, scanning documents or checking visa rules.

Who publishes these apps?

Krosz Inc. is the legal publisher. You can reach us at samayhuf@gmail.com or +1 213 396 3083. See the About page for company details.

Which app helps me sleep better?

Lulla - Sleep Sounds plays calming sounds for sleep. It is currently coming soon.

Which app should I use to scan documents?

ClearScan scans documents with no watermark and runs text recognition on your device. For QR codes and barcodes, use Scanwise. Both are in development.

Which app frees up phone storage?

Klenzer scans your phone for photos, videos and files that take up space, and deletes nothing until you unlock the fix. It is $3.99/month or $14.99 lifetime.

Is there one dashboard to manage all the apps?

No. Each app is a separate app with its own page, pricing and support. This site is the directory that lists them all.

How much do the apps cost?

Prices differ by app. The comparison table on this page lists the pricing for each one. Apple or Google shows the exact price in your country before you buy.

What data do the apps collect?

Each app collects only what it needs to work. Read the Privacy Policy or the policy page linked on each app page.
