About Alarm Klock

Alarm Klock is an alarm clock built to get you out of bed. Mission alarms keep you awake after you switch the alarm off, so you cannot just tap snooze and fall back asleep.

It was designed by studying what people dislike about other alarm apps and turning each complaint into a fix.

Alarms that need you awake

A normal alarm only asks you to touch a button. A mission alarm asks you to do something that needs you awake, which is the point. Alarm Klock is built around that idea.

How Alarm Klock works

  1. 1

Questions about Alarm Klock

What is a mission alarm?

An alarm that you can only stop by completing a task, so you are properly awake. Missions include math, memory, typing, Color clash, and movement tasks.

How much is it?

There is a 7-day free trial, then $2.99 a month or $19.99 a year. Subscriptions renew automatically until you cancel in Settings › your name › Subscriptions at least 24 hours before the period ends. Use Restore Purchases in the app if you reinstall.

How do I cancel or restore?

Cancel in your Apple ID subscription settings. To restore a purchase, open Alarm Klock and use Restore Purchases with the same Apple ID.
