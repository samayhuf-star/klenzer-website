About Pondly

Pondly is a water tracker with a penguin called Pip. Tap +150, +250 or +350 ml each time you drink and Pip reacts: he looks thirsty and weak when you are behind, and healthy and thriving when you reach your goal.

Alongside the daily goal there is a Thirst Forecast that adjusts for heat and workouts, Circles to share progress with friends, and a collection of penguins to unlock. The design is clean, light blue and friendly.

Meet Pip

Tracking water is easy to forget. Pip gives you a reason to open the app: drink regularly and he thrives, skip and he gets thirsty. A five-segment meter under him shows how well the day is going, with a short line such as how many ml are left to go.

Thirst Forecast

Your goal is not the same every day. The forecast starts from your base goal and adds water for hot weather and for workout time (none, 20, 40 or 60 minutes), then shows your goal for today. A "When to sip" chart suggests times across the day. Applying the forecast to your daily goal is part of Pondly Pro.

Circles with friends

Start a Circle, give it a name such as Office Pond, and share its 6-letter code. Friends join with the code. Everyone sees goal progress only, never what anyone actually drinks.

Collect Pips

You start with Pip. More penguins unlock as you log: Minty is available to use, Goldie unlocks at 10 litres logged, Lilac at a 7-day streak, Midnight at a 30-day streak and Cloud at 100 litres logged.

Questions about Pondly

What does the penguin do?

Pip shows how well you are hydrating: thirsty and weak when behind, healthy and thriving when you reach your goal.

What is the Thirst Forecast?

It adds water to your goal for heat and workouts and suggests when to sip. Applying it to your goal is a Pro feature.

What can my friends see in a Circle?

Only your goal progress, never what you drink.

How do I unlock more penguins?

Log litres and keep streaks: 10 litres, a 7-day streak, a 30-day streak and 100 litres unlock four more.

Can I delete my data?

Yes. Settings has a Delete all my data option.
