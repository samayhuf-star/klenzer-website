About Riseful

Riseful is an alarm clock built to get you out of bed. Mission alarms keep you awake after you switch the alarm off, so you cannot just tap snooze and fall back asleep.

It was designed by studying what people dislike about other alarm apps and turning each complaint into a fix.

Alarms that need you awake

A normal alarm only asks you to touch a button. A mission alarm asks you to do something that needs you awake, which is the point. Riseful is built around that idea.

Free for 7 days

You can use Riseful free for 7 days before choosing a plan.

How Riseful works
