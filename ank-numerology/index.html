About Ank Numerology

Ank Numerology reads your birth date and name through the Lo Shu grid, the 3 by 3 square used in Chinese and Indian numerology. Type your date of birth and you get your core numbers (Mulank, Bhagyank and your Personal Year), a free future reading and a grid that shows which digits you have and which are missing.

Every number comes with its working shown, so you can see how 14 becomes 5 or how a full birth date reduces to a single digit. When you want more, the Complete Report goes deep on your name, your past, present and future, and arrives as a long PDF in your inbox.

The numbers explained

TermWhat it means
Mulank (root number)Your birth day reduced to one digit. For a birth day of 14, 1 + 4 gives 5.
Bhagyank (destiny number)All digits of your full birth date added down to one digit.
Personal YearYour birth day and month plus the current year, reduced to one digit. It sets the theme of each year, and months follow it.
Lo Shu gridA 3 by 3 square filled with the digits of your birth date. Digits that repeat or are missing are read as strengths and gaps.
Planes and linesEight lines through the grid (thinking, feeling, practical, planning, willpower, action, determination, intuition), each marked full, partial or missing.

Questions about Ank Numerology

What is the Lo Shu grid?

It is a 3 by 3 square of numbers used in Chinese and Indian numerology. The digits of your birth date are placed on it, and the app reads which digits you have, which are missing and which lines are complete.

What do Mulank and Bhagyank mean?

Mulank is the root number from your birth day. Bhagyank is the destiny number from your full birth date. The app shows how each is worked out.

Is the reading free?

Yes. You get a short free reading with your core numbers, the grid and the next three months. The Complete Report is a one-time purchase and Ank Pro is a subscription.

Does Ank Pro include the Complete Report?

No. They are separate. The report is a one-time purchase and Pro is for unlimited checks, names and the full forecast.

Can I check someone else, like a family member?

Yes. Create a profile for each person and read their numbers separately.

Does it predict my future?

No. The numbers are prompts for reflection and entertainment, not predictions or advice.
