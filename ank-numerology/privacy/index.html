Ank Numerology Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026. Publisher: Samay Vashsht. Contact: samayhuf@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Profile details you enter (name, date of birth, language, interests). They are stored on your device. We do not receive them unless you buy the Name Report.
  • If you buy the Name Report: your email address, your name and date of birth and your chosen language are sent to our server to create the report and email it to you. We keep them only as long as needed to deliver the report, handle support and meet legal duties.
  • Purchases: handled by Apple and RevenueCat. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID and purchase status; if you buy the Name Report it also receives your email.
  • Notifications: daily numbers and optional Name Report tips are scheduled on your device. You can turn them off in the app settings or iOS settings.

What we do not do

We do not show ads, sell your data, or track you across other companies' apps.

Your choices

You can delete the data on your device from Settings, "Delete my data on this device". To ask us to delete data held on our server, email samayhuf@gmail.com.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13.

Disclaimer

Numerology is for reflection and entertainment. It does not predict events and is not medical, legal or financial advice.
