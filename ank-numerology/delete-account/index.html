Delete your Lo Shu Lab account

This page explains how to delete your account in Lo Shu Lab (also known as Ank Numerology), published by Samay.

Delete it inside the app

  1. Open the app and go to Account.
  2. Tap "Delete my account".
  3. Confirm with "Delete account and local data".

Or ask us by email

Email samayhuf@gmail.com from the address you signed in with, with the subject "Delete my account". We will delete it within 30 days and confirm by reply.

What is deleted and what is kept

Deleted: your email sign-in, your saved profiles (names and birth details) and pending orders. Kept: purchase and delivery records, which may be retained for legal, tax and support reasons. Subscriptions are managed by Google Play and are not cancelled by deleting your account; cancel them in Google Play first.
