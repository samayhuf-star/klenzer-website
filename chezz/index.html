About Chesz: City Chess League

Chesz is a chess app for playing against bots, from gentle beginner levels to very strong ones. There are 50 bot levels, so there is always an opponent at about your strength, and a harder one waiting when you improve. You can also play live online games against real players, with ratings.

Every game is played on one fixed 7-minute clock per side. That keeps games short and the rules the same for everyone, which is what makes the leaderboards fair.

Built for steady improvement

You start at a level that suits you and move up as you win. Because each level is a clear step, you can see your own progress instead of guessing. After each game you can step back and forward through the moves to see where it turned.

Leaderboards that mean something

Chesz ranks players by city, state, country and worldwide. Seeing your name near the top of your own city is a goal that is closer than a world ranking. City vs City seasons let your wins count for your city.

Play as a guest, or log in with email so your results and performance are tracked on your profile.

How Chesz: City Chess League works

  1. 1

    Choose a bot level

    Pick from 50 levels, beginner to brutal.

  2. 2

    Play a 7-minute game

    Each side has the same fixed clock. Bots reply after a 1-second beat, like a real opponent.

  3. 3

    Review the game

    Step back and forward through the moves.

  4. 4

    Climb the boards

    Win to climb city, state, country and world leaderboards.
