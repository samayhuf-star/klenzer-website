Privacy Policy

Photo Clutter Sorter ("the app") by Samay Vashsht. Last updated: 16 November 2026.

What the app does

The app reads screenshots in your photo library and sorts them into lists. Reading the text in each screenshot and choosing a list happens on your iPhone. Your photos and the text read from them are never uploaded to us or to anyone else.

Data stored on your device

Your lists, the list each screenshot is filed in, text read from screenshots, and the choices you make are stored in a local database on your device. Deleting the app, or using "Delete all data" in Settings, removes this data.

Photo library access

The app asks for access to your photo library only to find and read screenshots. You can choose to share all photos or selected photos, and you can change this in iOS Settings at any time.

Purchases

Subscriptions are handled by Apple and by RevenueCat, our subscription service provider. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID and purchase information (such as which plan is active) so the app can unlock paid features and restore purchases. We do not receive your payment details.

What we do not do

We do not collect your name, email, location or contacts. We do not use advertising or tracking SDKs. We do not sell or share your data. The app does not use artificial intelligence services.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above will be updated.
