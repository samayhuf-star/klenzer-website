About Photo Clutter Sorter

Photo Clutter Sorter sorts your screenshots and photos into lists you create. Sorting is rule-based and runs on your device, with no AI.

Make your own folders for shopping, articles, ideas and more. The design is a calm lavender and blue.

Screenshots pile up

Most people take screenshots to save something and never look again. Lists give each one a home so you can find it later.

How Photo Clutter Sorter works

  1. 1

    Create folders such as shopping, articles or ideas.
