About Stargaze

Stargaze is a sky map for your iPhone. Hold it up to see the stars, planets and constellations overhead, with no ads.

Free gives you stars to magnitude 4.5 and time travel of plus or minus one hour. Pro adds about 2,850 stars, 110 Messier objects, ISS tracking, a Tonight planner and plus or minus 12 hours of time travel.

How Stargaze works

  1. 1

    Align the sky

    Sky Align lines the map up with the real sky.

  2. 2

    Find an object

    Search and follow the arrow to it.

  3. 3

    Use camera or night mode

    Overlay the map on the camera, or switch to a red night mode.

  4. 4

    Plan the night

    Check the meteor shower calendar and save favourites.
