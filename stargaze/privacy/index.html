Stargaze Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026. Publisher: Samay Vashsht. Contact: samayhuf@gmail.com.

What the app uses

  • Location (while the app is open): to work out which stars and planets are above you. It is used on your device and is not sent to us.
  • Motion sensors and compass: to point the sky map where your phone points. Processed on your device only.
  • Camera: only for the optional camera-background view. Images are shown live and never saved or uploaded.
  • Satellite data: the app downloads the public ISS orbit data from CelesTrak. That request does not include your identity or location.
  • Favourites and settings are stored on your device.
  • Purchases: handled by Apple and RevenueCat. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase status.

What we do not do

We do not have accounts, show ads, sell your data, or track you across other companies' apps.

Your choices

You can turn off location, camera or motion access in iOS Settings at any time. Deleting the app removes the data stored on your device. Questions or deletion requests: samayhuf@gmail.com.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone.
