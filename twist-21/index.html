About Twist 21

Twist 21 is a card game that starts with blackjack and adds UNO-style action cards. You get the 21 rules you already know, plus cards that can flip a round in a single play.

Rounds are fast, so it fits a short break. The game is free to play: every action card and unlimited matches are included.

What makes it different

In plain blackjack, once the cards are dealt you mostly wait. In Twist 21 the action cards give you something to do: change what happens next, hurt the dealer's hand or rescue your own. That is the twist.

Chips, not cash

You play with chips and start with 10,000. Purchases in the app are for chips only; chips cannot be cashed out and there is no real-money gambling.
