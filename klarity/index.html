About Klarity

Klarity adds a short mindful pause before you open the apps that eat your day. Instead of opening straight into a feed, you stop for a moment and choose.

It works through iOS Shortcuts, so it does not need heavy permissions on your phone.

Awareness, not lock-down

Klarity does not shout at you or lock your phone. A pause is often enough to break the habit of opening an app without thinking. Screen-time awareness stays out of your way.
