Privacy, in plain language.

This page explains the website’s privacy practices and summarizes Klenzer’s published app policy. It does not replace the official policy.

Read the official Privacy Policy ↗

On-device processing

According to Klenzer’s published privacy policy, your photos, videos, and contacts are processed on your iPhone and never uploaded. The app has no account or sign-in and no analytics, advertising, tracking, or crash-reporting SDK. It does not collect a name, email address, device identifier, or payment card details.

Permissions are yours to control

Photo library permission lets Klenzer review media and measure locally stored files. Photos stored only in iCloud are not measured. Contacts access is optional and requested for Merge Contacts. Face ID is optional for unlocking Secret Album, and notifications are optional for local reminders. You can change permissions in iPhone Settings.

Klenzer does not request camera, microphone, current location, or tracking permission. Live Photo conversion can preserve location metadata already stored in a photo; it does not access your current location.

Secret Album and local storage

Secret Album holds photos only, encrypted on your iPhone and unlocked with Face ID or a PIN. Klenzer does not receive or store your face data. Secret Album is not a cloud backup: deleting the app deletes the album and its contents, and a forgotten PIN cannot be recovered.

The app stores preferences, the free daily action count, and scan-related data on your device. Its published policy describes these local records and how deleting the app removes them.

Purchases and support

In-app purchases are handled by Apple’s App Store. Klenzer states that it does not receive your payment card details or send purchase data to a third-party service. Apple handles purchases under its own privacy policies.

If you email support, the information you choose to include—such as your email address and description of the issue—is used to respond to your request. Do not send private photos, contact lists, your PIN, or payment details.

This landing website

This website has no sign-in, forms, analytics, advertising, tracking scripts, or third-party embeds. It does not set application cookies or store information in your browser. App images are served as part of the website rather than embedded from the App Store.

The website’s hosting provider may process standard connection information, such as your IP address and requested page, to deliver and protect the site. Following an App Store or legal-page link takes you to an external site with its own privacy practices. Using the support email opens your email service.

Official policy and questions

For the full app privacy policy, including permissions, retention, purchases, and the publisher’s contact details, visit the official Privacy Policy. The existing Terms of Use also remain in effect.
