Have a question about Klenzer? Start with these quick answers, or email the published support contact.

Deleted items still use space

Open Photos and find Recently Deleted. Restore anything you want to keep, then permanently delete the items you no longer need to reclaim space. Klenzer cannot empty Recently Deleted for you. Permanently deleted items cannot be restored from that album.

Reached the daily allowance?

Scanning and reviewing remain free. The 10 free items per day are shared across deleting photos and videos, merging contacts, and adding photos to Secret Album. You can wait for the next day or choose Pro to remove the daily limit. Live Photo conversion is Pro-only.

Manage or restore Pro

Manage or cancel monthly and yearly subscriptions in Settings → your name → Subscriptions. They renew automatically unless canceled at least 24 hours before the current period ends. The lifetime purchase does not renew. For an existing purchase, use Restore Purchases in Klenzer Settings or on the Pro screen with the Apple account that made the purchase.

Trouble accessing your photo library?

Check Klenzer’s Photos permission in iPhone Settings. The app can only review media you have allowed it to access. Photos stored only in iCloud are not measured.

Keep Secret Album safe

Secret Album is stored only on your iPhone. It is not a backup, and deleting Klenzer deletes the photos in it. A forgotten PIN cannot be recovered. Keep a separate copy of irreplaceable photos before removing them from Photos or deleting the app.
