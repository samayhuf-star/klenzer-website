Need a hand? Get help
Will Klenzer delete anything automatically?

No. You review the items and choose what to remove. Swiping left only marks a photo for deletion; swiping right keeps it. You confirm afterward, including your iPhone’s own deletion prompt. You can cancel instead of deleting.

Can I recover a photo after deleting it?

Deleted photos and videos go to Recently Deleted in the Photos app, where you can restore them for up to 30 days. If you permanently delete them there, they cannot be restored from Recently Deleted. Check your selection carefully before confirming.

Why hasn’t my available storage changed yet?

Deleted items still take up space while they are in Recently Deleted. To reclaim that space, open Photos, go to Recently Deleted, and permanently delete the items you no longer need. Klenzer cannot empty it for you. Restore anything you want to keep before emptying it.

What is free, and what needs Pro?

Scanning and reviewing are free. Without Pro, deleting photos and videos, merging contacts, and adding photos to Secret Album share one allowance of 10 free items each day—not 10 per feature. Pro removes that daily limit and adds Live Photo conversion to still images. Prices are shown in the app.

Does Klenzer upload my photos or track me?

According to Klenzer’s published privacy policy, photos, videos, and contacts are processed on your iPhone and are not uploaded. The app requires no account and has no tracking, ads, or analytics. Photos permission is needed to review your library; contacts access is optional for merging.

Are photos stored only in iCloud measured?

No. Photos stored only in iCloud are not included in Klenzer’s measurements. Your library size and the space you can reclaim depend on what is stored on your iPhone and what you choose to delete.

How do the Pro trials and subscriptions work?

Monthly and yearly subscriptions include a seven-day free trial for new subscribers and renew automatically unless canceled at least 24 hours before the current period ends. Manage or cancel in Settings → your name → Subscriptions. The lifetime option is a one-time purchase with no trial. All prices are shown in the app.

What should I know about Secret Album?

Secret Album stores photos only, encrypted on your iPhone and unlocked with Face ID or a PIN. It is not a cloud backup. Deleting Klenzer deletes Secret Album and its contents, and a forgotten PIN cannot be recovered. Keep a separate copy of photos you cannot afford to lose.

Does Klenzer clean system files or other apps?

No. Klenzer helps you review photos, videos, and duplicate contacts. It does not clear system files, caches, or other apps’ data, and it does not promise a faster phone or a specific amount of storage saved.
