SnapInfo Privacy Policy (updated 22 Nov 2026)

SnapInfo: Photo Link Scanner is published by Samay Vashsht.

What the app does with your photos
SnapInfo reads the photos you choose, on your phone, to find links, text, QR codes, phone numbers, email addresses and locations in them. Photos, and the text and details found in them, are processed on your device and are not uploaded to us. Scan results and notes are stored only on your phone.

Photo library access
The app asks for access to your photo library so it can read the photos you select. You can grant access to selected photos only, and change this any time in iOS Settings.

Location details
If a photo carries a GPS position, the app can show the place name for it. To do that, the coordinates are sent to Apple's geocoding service through iOS. They are not sent to us.

Accounts, ads and tracking
There is no account and no login. SnapInfo has no advertising and does not track you across other apps or websites.

Purchases
SnapInfo Pro is sold through Apple. We use RevenueCat to check whether a subscription is active. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID and purchase status. We never receive your name, email address or payment details. See RevenueCat's privacy policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

Sharing
When you share or export results, you send them yourself using the apps you choose.

Deleting your data
Deleting the app removes the scan results and notes stored on your phone.

Children
SnapInfo is not directed at children under 13.
