SnapInfo Support

For help with SnapInfo: Photo Link Scanner, email samayhuf@gmail.com and include your iPhone model and what happened.

How it works
Choose how many photos to scan and where from (all photos, screenshots or an album), then tap Scan. SnapInfo lists the links, text, QR codes and locations it finds. Everything is read on your phone.

Subscriptions
Manage or cancel SnapInfo Pro in your Apple ID account settings. Use Restore purchases in the app's Settings on a new device.
