About Ludo Meets Snake

This is a Ludo game built around one idea: honest dice. Every game locks in a secret seed before the first roll, and at the end you can check every single roll yourself, so nothing can be rigged. Inside the app it appears as "Ludo Honest" with the line "Dice you can verify".

You play 2 to 4 players against smart bots, race four tokens home and capture rivals to send them back. There are no ads between turns, no luck you can buy and no rubber banding, just classic Ludo played honestly.

Why verifiable dice

In many Ludo apps players suspect the dice are rigged, because the app controls the dice and the bots. Here, before the first roll the game commits to a secret seed and shows a fair-dice commitment code on the board. When the game ends you can check every roll against that commitment. That is how you know nothing changed halfway.

Played honestly

The game promises no rubber banding, which means bots do not get help when they are behind, and no luck you can buy. There are no ads between turns.

Try everything free for 7 days

The "What you get" screen offers a 7-day free trial with a fairness proof for every game, unlimited games against all bot levels, no ads and all boards and dice themes. You can cancel any time, or choose the free version.
