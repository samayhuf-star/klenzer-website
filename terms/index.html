The app

Klenzer works on your device. It finds duplicate or near-identical photos, screenshots, Live Photos and large videos, and lets you clear what you choose. Nothing is deleted automatically. You review every item and confirm. On iPhone, deleted items go to Recently Deleted; the space is freed when that album is emptied.

What is free and what is Pro

Klenzer is free to download. You can scan and review your library for free. There is a daily limit of 10 free action items, shared across deleting photos and videos, merging contacts, and adding photos to Secret Album. Klenzer Pro removes that daily limit. Live Photo conversion is a Pro feature.

Pro is offered as an auto-renewing subscription ($5.99/month or $29.99/year, with a 7-day free trial) or as a lifetime purchase ($29.99). Prices can differ by country and are shown by Apple or Google before you buy. Subscriptions renew automatically until you cancel in your Apple ID or Google Play account settings at least 24 hours before the period ends. A trial converts to a paid plan unless you cancel first. To restore a purchase after reinstalling, use Restore Purchases in Klenzer Settings or on the Pro screen with the same store account. Refunds are handled by Apple or Google.

No account

There is no account and no login.
