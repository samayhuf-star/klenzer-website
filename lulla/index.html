About Lulla - Sleep Sounds

Lulla - Sleep Sounds is an app for falling asleep faster and sleeping deeper. It plays calming sounds such as rain, ocean and soft ambient tones, built for the minutes when you are lying in bed with the lights off.

The interface is dark and low-glare on purpose, so opening the app at night does not wake you up. You can leave a sound playing all night.

Why a sleep sounds app

Many people fall asleep faster with steady background sound because it covers sudden noises such as traffic, neighbours or a partner moving. A constant sound gives the mind something simple to settle on instead of the day's thoughts.

Lulla keeps this simple. You pick a sound, set it to play, and put the phone down. There is no feed, no streak to keep and nothing to read.

Try before you pay

The first 3 minutes of every sound are free, so you can hear how each one actually feels before deciding. After that, a subscription unlocks the full library and all-night playback.
