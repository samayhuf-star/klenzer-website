About Photo Info Scanner

SnapInfo (Photo Info Scanner) reads your photos and pulls out the useful information hidden in them: website links, text, emails, phone numbers, QR codes, locations and camera details.

You choose how many photos to scan, newest first, and where from: all photos, Screenshots only, or a single album. Reading happens on your phone only and nothing is uploaded.

Scan only what you want

Choose 20, 50, 100, 250 or 500 photos, or type any number. Scan the whole library, just Screenshots, or one album. Results appear while the scan runs, and you can stop early and keep what was found.

What it finds in each photo

Links and websites from visible text and from QR codes, phone numbers and emails, place names and map coordinates when the photo has GPS, and camera details such as phone model, lens, date and settings.

Act on results straight away

Open a link, call or text a number, open a place on the map, copy or share any item, or add it to Notes or a Reminder. Select several photos and export everything to CSV.

Private by design

Photos are read on your phone and are never uploaded. SnapInfo asks for photo access once and explains why before the system prompt.

How Photo Info Scanner works

  1. 1

    Allow photo access

    Tap Continue on the permission screen. Nothing leaves your phone.

  2. 2

    Choose how many photos

    Pick a number and a source, then tap Scan.

  3. 3

    Review the results

    Search, filter by link, location or text, and open any photo for full details.

  4. 4

    Save or share

    Copy, share, add to Notes or Reminders, or export to CSV.
