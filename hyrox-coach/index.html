What you get with Hyrox Coach

  • 1

    Welcome to hybrid training: strength and endurance in one plan that fits your level, time and pace

  • 2

    AI coach (not a human trainer) that answers questions and adapts your session, with 20 free replies each month

  • 3

    Guided workouts with a step timer, rest built in and a short cool-down

  • 4

    Looping exercise demos you can see before each move, available offline

  • 5

    Momentum levels and weekly rhythm that reward showing up, one small step at a time

  • 6

    No account needed to start, and your workouts work offline
