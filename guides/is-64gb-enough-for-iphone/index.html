Is 64GB enough for an iPhone?

Updated 2026-11-20. By Klenzer Apps Menu names can differ slightly between iOS versions.

Short answer: 64GB is enough if you mostly message, browse and stream, and keep photos in iCloud or on a computer. It gets tight if you shoot lots of 4K video, download media or play big games. For most people who keep photos on the phone, 128GB is the safer choice.

Usable space is less than the number on the box

iOS, built-in apps and system files take part of the storage before you add anything. A 64GB phone therefore has noticeably less than 64GB free on day one. Check Settings › General › About for the capacity and iPhone Storage for what is used.

How much space photos and videos take

These are rough planning figures. Real sizes depend on the iPhone model, camera settings and what you shoot. Check Settings › Camera › Record Video, where iOS shows an approximate size per minute for each setting on your phone.

ItemRough sizeWhat it means
Typical photo (HEIC)About 2 to 3 MB1,000 photos is around 2 to 3 GB
1080p video, 30 fpsAbout 60 MB a minute1 hour is around 3.6 GB
4K video, 60 fpsAbout 400 MB a minute1 hour is around 24 GB
ProRAW photoOften 25 MB or more1,000 photos can pass 25 GB

Which size suits which person

You mostlyLikely fit
Message, browse, stream, keep photos in iCloud64GB can work, if you manage storage
Keep thousands of photos and some videos on the phone, play a few games128GB
Shoot lots of 4K or ProRAW, download films or music, keep big offline libraries256GB or more

This is a rule of thumb, not a guarantee. A phone you keep for many years also collects more apps and data over time.

If you already have 64GB

  • Turn on iCloud Photos with Optimize iPhone Storage. See how it works.
  • Stream instead of downloading, and offload unused apps.
  • Record video at 1080p unless you need 4K.
  • Review duplicates and screenshots regularly. See iPhone storage full: what to do.

Older and used phones

64GB iPhones are mostly older models you might find used or refurbished. Newer iOS versions and apps are larger than they used to be, so an old 64GB phone can feel fuller than the same size did when it was new.

Common questions

Is 64GB enough for iPhone in 2026?

It can be if you rely on iCloud and streaming. If you keep photos and videos on the phone, you will probably run out sooner than with 128GB.

How many photos fit in 64GB?

Roughly 15,000 or more if photos average 3 MB and nothing else used space, but apps and iOS take a large share, so the real number is lower.

Can I add storage to an iPhone later?

No. iPhone storage is fixed when you buy it. You can add cloud storage or external drives for some files.
