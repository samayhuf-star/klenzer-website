iPhone storage full and you can't delete photos

Updated 2026-11-20. By Sunny Vashisht Menu names can differ slightly between iOS versions.

Short answer: The usual causes are photos synced from a computer (these cannot be deleted on the phone), a nearly full phone that cannot process deletions, and items sitting in Recently Deleted. Restart, free a little space by offloading an app, then try again.

Check whether the photos were synced from a computer

Photos that were put on the iPhone by syncing with a Mac or PC through Finder or iTunes show up in synced albums. You cannot delete them on the phone. Remove them from the synced folder or album on the computer, then sync again, or turn off photo syncing in the device settings on the computer.

Free a small amount of space first

If the phone is completely full, deleting can fail or crawl. Offload one large app (Settings › General › iPhone Storage › the app › Offload App) to make room, then delete photos again.

Restart the iPhone

A restart clears many temporary glitches. After it comes back, open Photos and try deleting a small batch instead of thousands at once. Large selections can stall.

Empty Recently Deleted

Deleted photos do not free space until they are removed from Recently Deleted. Open Photos › Albums › Utilities › Recently Deleted › Select › Delete All. More detail in storage still full after deleting.

Check iCloud Photos

With iCloud Photos on, deletion syncs with iCloud. If the phone is offline or iCloud is busy, the delete can take time to complete. Connect to Wi-Fi and wait. Make sure you really want the photos gone from all devices first. See iCloud and iPhone storage.

Still stuck

  • Update iOS if an update is available and you have the space.
  • Back up the phone, then try again.
  • Contact Apple Support if Photos still refuses to delete items.

Common questions

Why is the Delete button missing for some photos?

The most common reason is that they were synced from a computer. Shared album items you did not add also cannot be deleted in the same way.

Is it safe to delete thousands of photos at once?

It can stall the phone. Delete in smaller batches and back up first.

Why does storage not change after I delete photos?

They are in Recently Deleted for up to 30 days. Empty that album to get the space back.
