How many photos can an iPhone hold?

Updated 2026-11-20. By Sunny Vashisht Menu names can differ slightly between iOS versions.

Short answer: With photos averaging about 3 MB, a phone could hold roughly 20,000 photos per 64GB if it held nothing else. After iOS, apps and other data, plan on well under that. These are estimates and your photo size may differ.

The maths

Divide storage by average photo size. These are best-case counts for photos only, using 1GB as about 1,000 MB and 3 MB per photo.

StorageBest case at 3 MBMore realistic
64GBAbout 21,000Often 8,000 to 12,000
128GBAbout 42,000Often 20,000 to 30,000
256GBAbout 85,000Often 45,000 to 60,000

The realistic column assumes iOS, apps and other data use a share of the space. It is an estimate, not a measurement.

Why your number will differ

  • Photo size varies with the camera, HEIC or JPEG, Live Photos and ProRAW.
  • Videos take far more space than photos. See is 64GB enough for size ranges.
  • Apps, messages and System Data share the same storage.

See your own average

Check Settings › General › iPhone Storage › Photos for the total used, then divide by the number of items shown in the Photos app to get your own average.

A shortcut for photo clutter

Klenzer for iPhone scans your library for duplicates, screenshots and large videos, and you decide what to remove. Pricing and limits are on the Klenzer page.

Common questions

How many photos fit on a 64GB iPhone?

Roughly 8,000 to 12,000 in practice if you also use the phone normally, and up to about 21,000 as a best case at 3 MB each.

Do Live Photos take more space?

Yes. A Live Photo includes a short video clip, so it takes more space than a still photo.
