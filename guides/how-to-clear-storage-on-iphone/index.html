How to clear storage on iPhone

Updated 2026-11-20. By Krosz Inc. Menu names can differ slightly between iOS versions.

Short answer: Clear storage one category at a time: apps and their data, photos and videos, Messages attachments, browser data and downloads. Each area has its own setting. Check iPhone Storage after each step so you know what worked.

Apps and app data

In Settings › General › iPhone Storage tap an app. Offload App removes the app but keeps data. Delete App removes both. Some apps also have their own clear-cache or downloads option inside the app.

Photos and videos

Delete what you do not need, then empty Recently Deleted. With iCloud Photos on, choose Optimize iPhone Storage so full-size originals live in iCloud. Details: iCloud and iPhone storage.

Messages

Open iPhone Storage › Messages. Review photos, videos and other large attachments and delete the ones you do not need. You can also set messages to keep for 30 days or 1 year in Settings › Messages, which deletes older ones.

Safari and downloads

Clear website data in Safari settings. Check the Files app › Downloads for old files. Note that clearing website data can sign you out of sites.

System Data

You cannot clear System Data directly. It usually shrinks after you free other space, update iOS or restart. See System Data explained.

A shortcut for photo clutter

Klenzer for iPhone scans your library for duplicates, screenshots and large videos, and you decide what to remove. Pricing and limits are on the Klenzer page.

Common questions

Is clearing storage the same as clearing cache?

Not exactly. Cache is temporary files. Clearing storage also covers apps, photos, messages and downloads, which are usually much bigger.

Can I clear storage without deleting my photos?

Yes. Offload apps, remove downloads, clear Messages attachments and use iCloud Photos with Optimize Storage.
