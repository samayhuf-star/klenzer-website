iPhone storage full: what to do

Updated 2026-11-20. By Krosz Inc. Menu names can differ slightly between iOS versions.

Short answer: Open Settings › General › iPhone Storage to see what is using the space. The biggest wins are usually photos and videos, large apps, and message attachments. Free a few GB there first, then deal with caches and System Data.

Step 1: See what is using the space

Go to Settings › General › iPhone Storage. Wait a few seconds while the bar finishes loading. The coloured bar splits your storage into groups such as Apps, Photos, Media, Messages, iOS and System Data. Below it is a list of apps sorted by size.

Look at the top three items. For most people it is Photos, one or two big apps, and Messages or WhatsApp. That tells you where to spend your time.

Step 2: Free space where it is biggest

If the biggest item isDo this
Photos or videosDelete duplicates, screenshots and long videos you no longer need. Then empty Recently Deleted, or the space does not come back. See storage still full after deleting.
A large appTap the app in the list and choose Offload App. This removes the app but keeps its documents and data, so you can reinstall later without losing them.
MessagesTap Messages in the storage list and use Review Large Attachments. You can also set Settings › Messages › Keep Messages to 1 year or 30 days.
WhatsAppClear large files from inside WhatsApp. See the WhatsApp storage guide.
System DataRestart your iPhone and clear Safari website data. See what System Data is and how to reduce it.

Step 3: Use the built-in recommendations

At the top of the iPhone Storage screen, iOS may show recommendations such as Offload Unused Apps, Review Large Attachments and Optimize Photos. They are safe to read through before you tap anything. Offloading unused apps keeps their data and only removes the app itself.

Step 4: Move photos off the phone safely

If photos are the problem, you have two sound options. Turn on iCloud Photos with Optimize iPhone Storage (Settings › your name › iCloud › Photos), or copy your photos to a computer and then delete them from the phone. Do not delete originals until you have confirmed the copy is somewhere else. If you use iCloud Photos, deleting a photo on the iPhone also deletes it from iCloud and your other devices. See iPhone storage full but you have iCloud.

Step 5: If it is still full

  • Restart the iPhone, then check the storage screen again. The numbers can take a few minutes to update.
  • Update iOS if an update is waiting and you have enough space for it.
  • As a last resort, back up the phone, erase it and restore from the backup. This clears many leftover temporary files, but only do it with a complete backup.

Common questions

Why does my iPhone say storage is full when I have few photos?

Photos are only one part of storage. Apps, their data, messages, downloaded media and System Data all count. Check the bar under Settings › General › iPhone Storage to see which group is largest.

Will deleting apps delete my data?

Deleting an app removes its data too. Offload App removes only the app and keeps its documents and data, which is safer when you only need space.

How much free space should I keep?

Aim for at least a few GB free. iOS needs room for updates, temporary files and the camera. Many people find the phone behaves better with 5 to 10 GB spare.

Does buying iCloud storage fix a full iPhone?

Not by itself. iCloud storage and iPhone storage are separate. iCloud only helps if you use it to hold photos or backups so you can remove copies from the phone.
