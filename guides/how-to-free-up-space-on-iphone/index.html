How to free up space on iPhone

Updated 2026-11-20. By Klenzer Apps Menu names can differ slightly between iOS versions.

Short answer: Start where the space is: open Settings › General › iPhone Storage, sort by size, then deal with the biggest items first. Large videos, unused apps, Messages attachments and offline downloads usually recover the most. Small cache clean-ups come last.

Check what is using space first

Open Settings › General › iPhone Storage. Wait for the bar to finish loading, then read the list from the top. iOS also shows recommendations here, such as offloading unused apps or reviewing large attachments. Menu names can differ slightly between iOS versions.

Gains depend on your own data. The order is a guide, not a promise.

Do the safe things first

  1. Back up what you care about before deleting anything.
  2. Offload apps rather than deleting them, so documents and data stay.
  3. Delete in small batches and check the storage bar after each step.

When deleting does not help

If space does not come back after deleting, or System Data is huge, see storage still full after deleting and what System Data is.

Common questions

What is the fastest way to free up space on iPhone?

Delete or move your largest videos and remove big offline downloads. Check iPhone Storage to see which items are largest on your phone.

Will offloading an app delete my data?

No. Offloading removes the app but keeps its documents and data, so reinstalling restores them.

Does clearing Safari data help much?

Usually only a little. Look at videos, downloads, messages and big apps first.
