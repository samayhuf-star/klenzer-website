Why is my iPhone storage still full after deleting everything?

Updated 2026-11-20. By Klenzer Apps Menu names can differ slightly between iOS versions.

Short answer: Deleted photos move to Recently Deleted and keep using space for up to 30 days. Open Photos › Albums › Utilities › Recently Deleted and choose Delete All. Then restart the iPhone and wait a few minutes for the storage figure to update.

The most common cause: Recently Deleted

When you delete a photo or video, iOS does not remove it right away. It moves it to the Recently Deleted album, where it stays for up to 30 days. It still uses storage there.

  1. Open Photos › Albums and scroll to Utilities › Recently Deleted. On older iOS versions the album sits directly under Albums. You may need Face ID or your passcode.
  2. Tap Select, then Delete All.

This permanently deletes those items, so make sure you do not need any of them.

Other places that hold deleted items

  • Files app. Open Files › Browse › Recently Deleted and delete items there.
  • Messages. Deleted conversations can sit in a Recently Deleted list on newer iOS versions. Check the Messages app.
  • Notes and Voice Memos. Both keep deleted items in a Recently Deleted folder for a while.

The storage bar has not refreshed

The figure under Settings › General › iPhone Storage can lag behind reality. Restart the iPhone, open the screen, and give it a couple of minutes to recalculate. If it still looks wrong, close Settings from the app switcher and open it again.

You use iCloud Photos

With iCloud Photos on, a deleted photo is removed from every device that uses the same iCloud account, and it lands in Recently Deleted on all of them. With Optimize iPhone Storage on, the iPhone keeps smaller versions and downloads originals when needed. See storage full but you have iCloud.

You deleted an app, but its data is still there

Deleting an app removes its data too. Offloading removes the app but deliberately keeps its documents and data, so the figure may barely change. Tap the app in iPhone Storage and choose Delete App if you want the data gone as well.

Common questions

How long do deleted photos stay in Recently Deleted?

Up to 30 days. After that iOS removes them automatically, but you can delete them sooner.

Can I get a photo back after emptying Recently Deleted?

Not from the phone. If you have an iCloud or computer backup made before you deleted it, you may be able to restore it from there.

Why is storage full when I deleted everything I could?

Photos are not the only thing using space. Check the storage bar for apps, messages and System Data. See System Data.
