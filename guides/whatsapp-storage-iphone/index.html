WhatsApp is filling my iPhone storage: how to free space

Updated 2026-11-20. By Sunny Vashisht Menu names can differ slightly between iOS versions.

Short answer: In WhatsApp, open Settings › Storage and Data › Manage Storage. Review the largest chats and files, then delete what you do not need. Also turn off automatic saving of received media to your photo library, so photos are not stored twice.

Check how much WhatsApp uses

Go to Settings › General › iPhone Storage on the iPhone and find WhatsApp in the list. The figure combines the app and all the media it stores. If it is several GB, a few busy group chats are usually the cause.

Clear large files from inside WhatsApp

  1. Open WhatsApp and go to Settings › Storage and Data › Manage Storage.
  2. Review the list of chats sorted by size, and the section for large files.
  3. Open a big chat and select the media to remove, or clear the chat's media.

Deleting media on your phone does not delete it for the other people in the chat. Only do this for files you do not need, since they cannot be recovered unless a backup or the sender still has them.

Stop photos being saved twice

If WhatsApp saves incoming photos and videos to your photo library, they use space in two places: in WhatsApp and in Photos. Open WhatsApp › Settings › Chats and switch off the option that saves incoming media to your library. You can also turn off it for individual chats from the chat info screen.

Reduce automatic downloads

Under Settings › Storage and Data, check the media auto-download options for photos, audio, videos and documents. Setting videos and documents to download only when you tap them stops big files from arriving by themselves.

Tidy the rest of your photos

WhatsApp often adds many near-duplicate photos, memes and screenshots to your library. Review those in Photos, or use a cleaner such as Klenzer to find duplicates, screenshots and large videos so you can choose what to remove. Then empty Recently Deleted, see why storage can stay full after deleting.

Common questions

Will clearing storage delete my chats?

Clearing media removes the photos, videos and files, not the text of your messages, unless you choose to delete the whole chat.

Does a WhatsApp backup use iPhone storage?

The backup is stored in iCloud, not on the phone. It uses iCloud space, which is separate from your iPhone storage.

Why does WhatsApp show more storage than my messages need?

Most of the space is media: photos, videos, voice notes and documents. Text messages take very little.
