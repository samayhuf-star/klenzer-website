Why is my iPhone storage full when I have iCloud?

Updated 2026-11-20. By Krosz Inc. Menu names can differ slightly between iOS versions.

Short answer: iCloud storage is space online. iPhone storage is the space on the phone itself. Paying for more iCloud does not free space on the phone unless iCloud Photos is on with Optimize iPhone Storage, so originals can be replaced by smaller versions on the device.

Two different storages

iPhone storageiCloud storage
Where it isOn the phoneOn Apple's servers
Where to checkSettings › General › iPhone StorageSettings › your name › iCloud
What fills itApps, photos, videos, messages, System DataBackups, iCloud Photos, iCloud Drive, mail
How you add moreDelete or move things, or buy a bigger phoneUpgrade your iCloud plan

A free iCloud account includes 5 GB. A phone can be almost full while iCloud has plenty of room, and the other way round.

Set iCloud Photos to save phone space

  1. Go to Settings › your name › iCloud › Photos.
  2. Turn on Sync this iPhone (called iCloud Photos on older versions).
  3. Choose Optimize iPhone Storage.

With this on, the iPhone keeps smaller versions and downloads full-size photos when you open them. If you pick Download and Keep Originals, nothing is saved on the phone.

Know what deleting does with iCloud Photos

iCloud Photos is a sync service, not a backup. Deleting a photo on the iPhone deletes it from iCloud and your other devices too, and it sits in Recently Deleted for up to 30 days. To free phone space without losing photos, use Optimize iPhone Storage instead of deleting.

Wait for the upload to finish

Photos upload before the phone can replace originals. On a slow connection or with Low Power Mode on, uploading can take a long time. Connect to Wi-Fi and power, keep Photos open for a while, and check progress at the bottom of the Photos library screen.

Things iCloud does not shrink

Optimizing photos does not reduce apps, downloaded media, messages or System Data. If those are the largest items, see System Data and iPhone storage full: what to do.

A shortcut for photo clutter

Klenzer for iPhone scans your library for duplicates, screenshots and large videos, and you decide what to remove. Pricing and limits are on the Klenzer page.

Common questions

Should I buy more iCloud storage?

Only if iCloud itself is full, for example it says you cannot back up or upload photos. It does not directly free space on the iPhone.

Can I use iCloud as a backup and still free the phone?

Yes. Back up the phone to iCloud, use Optimize iPhone Storage for photos, and delete large files you no longer need on the device.

What happens if iCloud is full?

New photos stop uploading and backups can fail. Free iCloud space or upgrade your plan to resume.
