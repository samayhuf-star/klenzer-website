What happens when your iPhone storage is full?

Updated 2026-11-20. By Krosz Inc. Menu names can differ slightly between iOS versions.

Short answer: When storage is full, the iPhone cannot save new photos, videos or files, may fail to install apps or iOS updates, and can slow down or crash apps. Your existing data is not deleted automatically, but you will see warnings until you free space.

What stops working

AreaWhat you may notice
CameraA message that storage is full, or photos and videos fail to save
AppsCannot install or update apps; apps crash or lose changes when saving
iOS updatesUpdate will not download or install without enough free space
MessagesAttachments fail to download or send
BackupsBackups can fail if the phone cannot create temporary files
SpeedGeneral slowness while the system struggles for working space

Does the iPhone delete anything on its own?

It does not delete your photos, messages or apps. iOS may clear some caches and temporary files when it needs room, which is why the figures can change by themselves. Your own content stays until you remove it.

Warning signs before it is completely full

  • A banner saying storage is almost full.
  • Downloads or updates stalling.
  • The Camera warning when you try to record.
  • Photos and Messages loading slowly.

How much space you need back

Free at least a few GB so iOS can work normally, and more if you need to install an update. Start with the largest items under Settings › General › iPhone Storage. See iPhone storage full: what to do for the step-by-step order.

Prevent it happening again

  • Use iCloud Photos with Optimize iPhone Storage, or copy photos to a computer regularly.
  • Set Messages to keep messages for 1 year instead of forever.
  • Offload apps you rarely use.
  • Review duplicates and screenshots every month, by hand or with a cleaner app.

Common questions

Can a full iPhone lose my photos?

Not because it is full. The risk is losing photos when you delete things in a hurry. Back up before clearing space.

Will my iPhone still make calls when storage is full?

Usually yes. Calls and basic functions keep working, but apps and updates can fail.

Does a full iPhone damage the battery?

No. Low storage does not harm the battery, although a struggling phone can feel slower.
