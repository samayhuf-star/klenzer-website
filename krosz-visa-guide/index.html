About Krosz Visa Guide

Krosz Visa Guide answers one question quickly: do I need a visa? Pick the country your passport is from, type where you want to go, and get a clear answer, with the visa type and how long you can stay. The tagline is "Know before you go."

It is free, needs no login and is simple enough for any age to use. Besides the visa check it has a Visa search that lists results by destination and a Photo checker for visa photos.

Answers you can read in seconds

Results are colour-coded so you can scan them: green for No visa needed, amber for Depends on your case and red for Visa needed. Tap a destination to read the detail, including the purpose of travel, the visa type and the allowed stay.

Checked, never guessed

The app says it only shows countries where it holds checked information, and that it never guesses. The data comes from the Krosz visa database and is checked against official sources where shown. The app also reminds you to always confirm before you travel.

Visa photo checker

Pick a photo from your phone and the app checks its size, shape and file size. The photo stays on your phone. It does not check face position or background colour, and each country has its own photo rules.

Questions about Krosz Visa Guide

Is it free?

Yes, and there is no login.

What does "Depends on your case" mean?

The answer depends on your situation, for example purpose of travel, so read the detail and confirm with the official source.

Does the photo checker upload my photo?

No. The photo stays on your phone.

Does the photo checker check my face or background?

No. It checks size, shape and file size only.

Can it guess a country it does not know?

No. It only shows countries where it holds checked information.

Which passports can I choose?
