About Momentum – Men’s Workout

Momentum – Men's Workout is a sleek, minimal workout app. Exercise demos use clear animated GIFs so you can see the move before you do it.

Onboarding asks your goal, height and weight to suggest the right exercises, and there are exercises for every body part, from beginner to advanced.

Simple and light

The design is light and minimalist with a coral accent. The aim is to get you from opening the app to your first set with as little fuss as possible.
