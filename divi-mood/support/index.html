Common questions

Restore a purchase. Open Settings and tap Restore purchases.

Manage or cancel my subscription. Open Settings, tap Manage subscription, or go to your Apple ID subscriptions in the Settings app.

Notifications are not arriving. Allow notifications for Divi Mood in your iPhone Settings, then check the reminder options in the app.

Add a partner. Ask them to tap Share with your partner in Settings, then paste the code on the Add a person screen.

Delete my data. Everything stays on your device. Deleting the app removes it.
