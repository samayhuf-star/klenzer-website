Divi Mood Privacy Policy (updated 17 Nov 2026)

Divi Mood stores everything you enter (names, period dates, flow, moods, settings) only on your phone. There is no account, no advertising, no analytics on your cycle data, and nothing is sold or shared.

Notifications: mood alerts are scheduled locally on your phone if you allow them.
Purchases: handled by Apple and RevenueCat. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID, purchase status and basic subscription attributes (such as number of people tracked). It does not receive your cycle dates or moods.
Sharing: only what you choose is sent through the iOS share sheet (a mood card, or a short code with a last period date and cycle length).
Delete: Settings > Delete all my data, or delete the app.
Children: not directed to children under 13.
Medical: for general wellness only; not a medical device or birth control. Predictions are estimates and can be wrong.
