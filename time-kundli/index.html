About Time Kundli

Time Kundli turns your birth details into a Vedic kundli and shows your future as colour-coded blocks, so you can see at a glance which stretches call for patience and which are good for a big move.

You can also run a match-making (Kundli Milan) report for two people. Birth details stay on your phone. The app is available in English and Hindi.

How Time Kundli works

  1. 1

    Enter birth details

    Add date, time and place of birth for yourself or someone else.

  2. 2

    Read your chart

    See your ascendant, moon sign, nakshatra and Mangal dosha.

  3. 3

    Check the future blocks

    Each period is coloured from Hold tight to Go big.

  4. 4

    Match two kundlis

    Run a match-making report for two people and export it as a PDF.
